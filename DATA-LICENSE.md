# Data license

TV schedule, show metadata, alternate titles, and the derived RSS feed in
`site/data/finales.json` and `site/feed.xml` come from
[TVMaze](https://www.tvmaze.com/) and is available under the
[Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

Drama Ready filters the source data by language and show type, derives season
completion and episode-length classifications, and adds search shortcuts. The
adapted data remains available under CC BY-SA 4.0. Each record links to its
TVMaze source. Poster images are served by TVMaze; no ownership of them is claimed.

The application source code is separately licensed under the MIT License.
